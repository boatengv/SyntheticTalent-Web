# Synthetic Talent design brief

- **Design read:** brands, platforms and future partners meeting an agency of AI creators; the register is a live broadcast studio: dark, precise, a little theatrical, never hype.
- **Concept spine:** stage/spotlight, "On air". The site is the studio where a signal becomes a creator. Every text block is set like an AI Cleared lower third (dark box, mint bar on the left), the same chrome our creators wear on TikTok, YouTube and Instagram.
- **Delivery tier:** cinema (scroll-scrubbed film hero, scroll chapters, transform-only reveals).
- **Locked palette:** ground `#0F0F12`, raised `#17181D`, panel `#24262E`, ink `#F4F5F7`, muted `#9A9DA8`, hairline `#2A2C34`, accent mint `#34D399` (fills, bars, focus) and `#0E8F63` (accent text on light). Defense: this is the user's own AI Cleared brand palette (explicit brand colours), which overrides the near-black + green ban. One accent only.
- **Light variant (user constraint):** ground `#F3F4F2`, raised `#FFFFFF`, ink `#0F0F12`, muted `#5B5E68`, hairline `#DADCE0`; the film stage stays dark in both themes because it is the studio.
- **Locked type:** Poppins 600/700 display and 400/500 body (AI Cleared's typeface), JetBrains Mono 500 for figures, dates and handles. No serif.
- **Animation mode: animated-website**
- **Journey shape:** single-shot. One ~15s continuous take.
- **Journey (4 chapters over the one film):**
  1. Establishing (0-25%): dark studio, mint filaments drifting in from both edges. "Virtual creators, managed like real talent." Kicker "Launching soon". CTA "Meet the roster".
  2. Convergence (25-50%): filaments braid into one tall vertical light panel. "Every creator starts as a signal." Persona, voice, niche.
  3. The screen (50-75%): the panel resolves into a glowing 9:16 screen on a glossy floor. "Made for the vertical screen." TikTok, YouTube, Instagram.
  4. The lineup (75-100%): two more screens light up beside it under one overhead spot. "A roster, run like an agency." CTA "See the results".
- **World grammar:** photoreal studio film, 35mm lens, eye level rising slightly, pure dark ground `#0F0F12`, mint `#34D399` emissive light only, soft white overhead spot, glossy black floor with soft reflections, no people, no faces, no text, locked exposure, no cuts, constant slow forward push.
- **How the journey enacts the spine:** the visitor's scroll literally brings a creator "on air": noise becomes a signal, the signal becomes a screen, the screen becomes a roster.
- **Mobile framing:** subject always centre-safe (middle 40% of width); mobile encode capped at 720p; copy boxes sit in the lower third.
- **Delivery budget:** desktop clip <= 32 MiB, mobile clip <= 16 MiB.
- **Combinatorial pick:** Deep Dark with a twist (broadcast lower-third chrome) · full-bleed cinematic imagery then solid with soft ambient depth · clean geometric grotesk (Poppins) · cinematic hero with text bottom-left over image · poster-stacked storytelling · signature components: layered image crop frames (roster), oversized metrics strip (results), hover-accordion slices (services), vertical rhythm lines (roadmap) · second-read: one oversized numeral (9,362) as structure.
- **Section plan:** 1 Journey hero (film + 4 chapters) · 2 Roster (layered crop frames, asymmetric) · 3 Results (oversized numeral + metrics strip + ruled table) · 4 What we do (accordion slices) · 5 Roadmap (vertical rhythm line) · 6 Team (two-up, one ruled) · 7 Legal (two text columns) · footer.
- **Eyebrow budget:** ceil(7/3) = 3: hero kicker, Results period, Roster note.
- **Asset plan:** storyboard + scroll film (desktop + mobile encodes + posters), 6 section boards in `refs/`, monogram mark + favicon set, custom service icon set (3 glyphs), one atmospheric section plate, OG card 1200x630. Creator imagery is the existing Higgsfield character art (Juno, Wren, AI Cleared), always labelled AI-generated.
- **CTA inventory:** "Meet the roster" (lower-third bar button, bar grows on hover) · "See the results" (inline underline + arrow) · top-post links (row hover with mint rail) · Wren clip Play/Pause (round glass control) · nav links (mono, current section marked by mint tick).
- **Honesty rules:** results are AI Cleared's real figures only, with the period stated; no invented stats; every creator image and video labelled AI-generated; no em dashes.
