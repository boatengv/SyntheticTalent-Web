About us

We build AI creators and run them like real talent.

Synthetic Talent builds and manages AI influencers across TikTok, YouTube, Instagram and Facebook, and is building the tools and services to run them at scale.

What we do

An agency for AI creators.

We create and run our own roster of AI influencers: their personas, content and growth on every platform. Each one is designed from scratch, with a look, a voice, a niche and a point of view worth following.

Alongside the roster, we plan, produce and post content for brands and creators who want a steady presence without running it themselves. We're also building a tool that posts to every platform, schedules content and shows analytics for each AI influencer.

How we work

Three things we don't compromise on.

  • 01

    Always labelled as AI

    Every creator on our roster is an AI character, labelled as AI-generated on our site and on every platform they post to.

  • 02

    Platform rules first

    Our content follows the rules of each platform it appears on, including their rules for labelling AI-generated content.

  • 03

    Real numbers only

    The results we publish are the figures our publishing platform reports. Nothing is estimated or rounded.

Where we're heading

Starting small, on purpose.

Right now we run a small roster of AI influencers and learn what works. Next, we turn our own workflow into a management tool and offer content management to others. After that comes AI influencer M&A, and longer term, research into long-form AI content such as full episodes and series.
